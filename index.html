Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Surron e-bike? +
A Surron is a lightweight electric dirt bike designed for off-road adventures. It has a 60V electric motor, can reach 80+ km/h in Sport mode, and is perfect for both beginners and experts (power is adjustable!). Zero emissions, maximum fun.
Do I need experience to ride? +
Not at all! ECO mode is designed for complete beginners. Our professional guide will teach you everything you need to know. Many of our customers have never ridden an e-bike before. We match the difficulty to YOUR level.
What's the difference between the three modes? +
It's the same powerful motor, but the power output is adjustable. ECO = controlled power for beginners. STANDARD = balanced power for intermediate riders. SPORT = full power for experts (80+ km/h). Your guide controls which mode is best for you.
What's included in the price? +
€100 per person includes: the 1-hour tour, professional helmet, safety gear, expert guide, full insurance, safety briefing, route choice, and photos (if you want). Nothing extra to pay during the tour.
Is it safe? +
Yes! All riders get professional helmets, safety briefing, and expert guides. Bikes are regularly maintained. We match the route difficulty to your skill level. Safety is our #1 priority.
What if the weather is bad? +
We operate in light rain (e-bikes are waterproof!). For severe weather, you can reschedule to another date or get a full refund. We'll give you 24-hour notice if we need to cancel.
Can I book for a group? +
Absolutely! Groups of 3+ people get special rates. We also offer custom private tours for team building, family reunions, or bachelor parties. Contact us for custom group pricing.
Do I need a driver's license? +
No! Our tours take place on a private 200-hectare property. No public roads, no driver's license required. Everyone can ride legally and safely with our expert guides.
What safety gear is provided? +
Professional helmet, body protection, elbow pads, and knee pads are all included in every tour. If you prefer to use your own gear, that's perfectly fine too - we just want you safe and comfortable!
Tell me about the 200-hectare property +
Our private 200-hectare estate is a true adventure playground. Beyond the trails, we produce organic wine and high-quality olive oil on-site. The scenic vineyard and forest terrain provides four different route difficulty levels - from family-friendly scenic rides to expert technical challenges. It's a complete experience!
Can I buy the organic wine or olive oil? +
Yes! We produce organic wine and olive oil on-site. After your tour, you're welcome to purchase directly from our production. Ask your guide for current availability and pricing.
