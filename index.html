The Machine

The Surron Light Bee

The bike that changed off-road riding. Fully electric, almost silent, and light enough that a first-timer can pick it up — but quick enough to keep an experienced rider grinning.

Easy to ride. Hard to put down.

No clutch. No gears. No noise. You twist the throttle and go — which is exactly why people who have never touched a motorbike are riding confidently within five minutes.

At around 55 kg it is roughly a third of the weight of a petrol enduro bike. That means it is far less intimidating to handle, easy to catch if it leans, and genuinely forgiving when you are still learning.

One model. That's it.

We ride the Surron Light Bee and nothing else. Every rider on every tour is on the same bike — we simply change the power mode to match your level. No confusing options, no upsells, no "premium" bike for extra money.

Same Bike, Your Pace

Three Power Modes

The Light Bee has adjustable power built into the motor. Your guide sets the mode that fits your experience — and can change it during the tour as your confidence grows.

Eco

Never ridden before? Start here.

  • Power limited to a gentle, predictable level
  • Throttle response softened — no surprises
  • Ideal for complete beginners and younger riders
  • Relaxed, scenic pace with plenty of photo stops
  • Your guide can unlock more power when you're ready

Standard

Comfortable on a bike already?

  • Balanced power — noticeably quicker than Eco
  • Enough torque for climbs and loose terrain
  • Opens up the technical forest and off-road routes
  • Great for anyone with MTB, scooter or quad experience
  • Still fully guided, still controlled

Sport

You know what you're doing.

  • Full power, nothing held back
  • Instant electric torque from a standstill
  • Unlocked on the advanced routes only
  • For riders with real off-road experience
  • Guide's discretion — earned, not requested
One 60V motor. Three settings. Zero compromise.

This is not three different bikes — it is the same Surron Light Bee with the power dialled in for you. It's why a nervous first-timer and their off-road-mad partner can ride the same tour together and both have the day of their life.

Choose Your Terrain

Four Routes, Four Levels

Every route runs entirely on our private estate. You pick the difficulty when you book — and if it turns out to be too easy or too much, we change it on the spot.

Level 1 · Beginner

The Vineyard Route

Wide, smooth dirt tracks winding between the vines with the mountains on one side and the Mediterranean in the distance. Gentle gradients, excellent visibility, and the best photos of the day.

Best for: First-timers, families, nervous riders · Mode: Eco
Level 2 · Intermediate

The Forest Trail

Into the pines on narrower singletrack. Roots, dips, dappled light and enough corners to make you feel like a rider. A real step up from the vineyard without ever becoming intimidating.

Best for: Anyone who's ridden something before · Mode: Eco → Standard
Level 3 · Advanced

Off-Road Challenge

Rock, loose shale, steep climbs and proper line choice. This is where the Light Bee's low weight and instant torque start to matter — and where you find out how good your balance really is.

Best for: Experienced off-road riders · Mode: Standard → Sport
Level 4 · Expert

Advanced Forest

Our hardest terrain. Technical rock gardens inside dense forest, tight switchbacks and steep, committed descents. Full Sport mode. We'll be honest with you about whether you're ready.

Best for: Skilled riders only · Mode: Sport
Where You Ride

200 Hectares. Entirely Private.

Not a public trail. Not a shared bike park. A working organic estate in the Tarragona hills that we have to ourselves.

No traffic. No licence. No limits.

Because every metre of trail sits on private land, there are no public roads involved — which is exactly why you do not need a driving licence to ride with us. No plates, no paperwork, no age-18 rule.

It also means no cars, no hikers to dodge and no other bikes on the trail. When you're out there, the estate is yours.

The terrain runs from flat vineyard tracks all the way up into rocky forest — which is how we fit four genuinely different route levels into one property.

Organic wine & olive oil, grown on these fields

The estate isn't a theme park — it's a working farm. The vines you ride between and the olive groves around you are harvested every year, and the grapes and olives are turned into organic wine and extra virgin olive oil by a local winemaker on the neighbouring estate.

After your tour you're welcome to taste and buy directly from the production. Ask your guide what's available on the day — it's the kind of souvenir that beats a fridge magnet.

Ride through it. Then take some home.

Before You Come

Good to Know

The practical details, so there are no surprises on the day.

Who can ride

  • From 12 years old with a parent or guardian present. If they can confidently ride a mountain bike, they can handle Eco mode
  • From 15 years old riders can come without a parent — we just need written permission from a parent or guardian
  • Every rider goes out with a professional guide, whatever their age
  • No driving licence needed — it's private land
  • No experience needed — Eco mode is built for day one
  • Rider height from roughly 1.45 m so you can reach the ground comfortably
  • Maximum rider weight 100 kg
  • Reasonable general fitness — you'll be standing on the pegs at times

What to wear & bring

  • Closed shoes — trainers are fine, boots are better. No sandals
  • Long trousers and long sleeves — protects against dust and scrub
  • Sunglasses or clear glasses if you wear them
  • Water, and sun cream in summer
  • Clothes you don't mind getting dusty — you will get dusty
  • Your own helmet or armour if you prefer it (optional)

Practical details

  • Location: private estate in the Tarragona countryside — exact address and directions sent when your booking is confirmed
  • Duration: 1 hour riding, allow around 1.5 hours on site including briefing and kitting up
  • Availability: seven days a week, weekends book up first
  • Languages: English, Español, Nederlands, Català
  • Payment: on the day — no deposit required
  • Cancellation: free up to 24 hours before your slot
  • Groups: small groups and private bookings welcome — just ask
