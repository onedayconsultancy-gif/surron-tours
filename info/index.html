What it's actually like.

There is no engine noise. You hear your own tyres on the gravel, the wind, and the birds — and then you open the throttle and the bike simply goes. No gears. No clutch. No lever to get wrong. You twist, and it pulls.

Most people are riding properly within five minutes. Not creeping along behind a guide — actually riding, choosing their own line, laughing inside their helmet.

And there is nobody else out there. This is not a public trail or a shared bike park. It is a working organic estate — 200 hectares of vines, olives and pine — and while you are on it, it is yours. No traffic. No queue. No other group waiting for their turn.

The bike: a Surron Light Bee.

Fully electric, near-silent, and about 55 kg — roughly a third of a petrol enduro bike. No clutch, no gears: you twist the throttle and go. Every rider is on the same bike; we set the power mode to match you. Eco if you have never ridden, Sport if you know what you are doing, and your guide can switch you over mid-ride once you are comfortable.

Getting here.

We are on a private estate in the Tarragona countryside, in Catalonia. Because it is private land we don't publish the address — you get the exact location, a map pin and driving directions as soon as your date is confirmed. There is parking on site.

No car?

We can come and pick you up. It costs extra on top of the ride and it adds a bit of travel time, so message us with where you are staying and we'll tell you what it would be before you book anything.

Who can ride

  • From 12 years old with a parent or guardian present. If they can confidently ride a mountain bike, they can handle Eco mode
  • From 15 years old riders can come without a parent — we just need written permission from a parent or guardian
  • Every rider goes out with a professional guide, whatever their age
  • No driving licence needed — it's private land
  • No experience needed — Eco mode is built for day one
  • Rider height from roughly 1.45 m so you can reach the ground comfortably
  • Maximum rider weight 100 kg
  • Reasonable general fitness — you'll be standing on the pegs at times

What to wear & bring

  • Closed shoes — trainers are fine, boots are better. No sandals
  • Long trousers and long sleeves — protects against dust and scrub
  • Sunglasses or clear glasses if you wear them
  • Water, and sun cream in summer
  • Clothes you don't mind getting dusty — you will get dusty
  • Your own helmet or armour if you prefer it (optional)
